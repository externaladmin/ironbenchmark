Why we're asking for your thoughts

How you respond matters more than it used to. Customers can find information anywhere now — increasingly by asking AI — so your response is one of the few things still in your control.

Phone calls and walk-ins are only part of the picture. Customers reach you through web forms, Facebook Messenger, text and listing platforms.

A missed inquiry isn't a lost order. It's a lost machine, and every parts and service dollar that machine would have earned for the rest of its life.

Nobody has published what good looks like. This is the first benchmark of its kind. 17 quick questions. About 5 minutes.

What's in the report
  • Channel coverage — which dealers are active on which channels
  • Response speed benchmarks — average first-response times by channel
  • Who owns the lead — how dealers assign and manage incoming inquiries
  • Follow-up behavior — what happens after a quote goes out
  • Reputation management — how dealers monitor and generate reviews
